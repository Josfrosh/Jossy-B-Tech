Application follow-through checklist

Practical toolkit

Keep a backup of the vacancy link and reference, role and employer name, the CV and cover letter submitted, application date, confirmation and next follow-up date. Tailor each application, prepare for interviews, and follow up through the employer’s official channel. These steps help you stay organized; they do not create a company introduction or guarantee a response, interview or job.
